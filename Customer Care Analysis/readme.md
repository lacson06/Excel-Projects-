**Customer Care Analysis**
  **Project Overview**

This Excel project demonstrates end-to-end data analysis workflows—including data cleaning, transformation, dashboard design, and strategic business reporting—using call center operations data. It highlights actionable insights and recommendations to improve customer satisfaction and optimize revenue performance.

  **Workbook Architecture**

Commet_Comm_Data: Raw operational data extracted directly from the call center database.

CLEANED_Data: Transformed and standardized dataset prepared for analysis, with handled missing values and structured data types.

DATA_CHARTS: Pivot tables and aggregations supporting dynamic charts and high-level summaries.

PROFILE: Customer demographic and behavioral analysis, featuring visual trend summaries, critical takeaways, and actionable operational recommendations.

REVENUE: Financial performance overview broken down by customer segment, complete with revenue visualizations, trend analysis, and strategic growth opportunities.
